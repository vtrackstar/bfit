Before you begin

Choose movements you can practice safely. Stop for pain, pinching, numbness, tingling or unusual instability. Seek qualified advice for persistent or worsening symptoms, exercise restrictions or uncertainty. This is general fitness training, not rehabilitation or medical clearance.

Choose your starting point

If you already use Gamer Fit, keep your movement breaks. Add targeted practice only when you have a specific goal that your current routine doesn't meet. You can use this workout without reading either guide.

Movements I already practice adequately (complementary mode only)

All initial exercises use a chair, wall, or sturdy support as appropriate. No floor work required.

My workout

Start with one set of each movement. Rest as needed and finish when your available time is up. Record each completed practice once; do not rush to finish.

How this fits with Gamer Fit

Keep short movement breaks during gaming. If you want to improve a movement over time, practice it here. A comfortable movement break doesn't automatically replace challenging strength work, and a targeted workout doesn't replace getting up regularly during a long seated day.
