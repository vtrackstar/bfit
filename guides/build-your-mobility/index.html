Before you begin

Choose movements you can practice safely. Stop for pain, pinching, numbness, tingling or unusual instability. Seek qualified advice for persistent or worsening symptoms, exercise restrictions or uncertainty. This is general fitness training, not rehabilitation or medical clearance.

Choose your starting point

If you already use Gamer Fit, keep your movement breaks. Add targeted practice only when you have a specific goal that your current routine doesn't meet. You can use this workout without reading either guide.

What about slow lowering and eccentric strength?

When you lower into a chair, lower your heels from a calf raise or move into a supported lateral lunge, muscles can lengthen under tension to control the movement. This is eccentric strength practice. Choose strength-oriented movements and practice the easy version first. Once ready, controlled lowering is one option, not a requirement. If your existing strength routine already covers the movement, do not add unnecessary sets. Learn how lowering, holding and lifting work together.

Movements I already practice adequately (complementary mode only)

All initial exercises use a chair, wall, or sturdy support as appropriate. No floor work required. Strength-oriented practice is optional; dumbbells are only suggested for people already familiar with lifting. Existing strength workouts may already cover these movements.
