06 / Four-week discovery

What to try over the next four weeks

You can spend another week on an activity you like. If a session feels too hard, shorten it or ease the pace.

Week 1

Get comfortable

Try your starting routine. Learn where you can move safely and what a comfortable pace feels like.

Week 2

Try something new

Explore one class, sport, or cardio option you're curious about. You can observe a class before joining.

Week 3

Build a little

If you've been recovering well, consider adding five minutes to one or two comfortable cardio sessions or a small amount of strength practice.

Week 4

Decide what stays

Which activity would you do again next week? Give it a spot on your calendar and think back to the everyday tasks you picked at the start.

What if you're already active? Use the builder as a weekly check. Look for gaps in balance or strength, not just more exercise. You can skip the discovery weeks entirely.
