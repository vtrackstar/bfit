## Programming
Goal: one overhand still dead-hang pull-up, straight arms, chin above bar without kicking or neck reach, controlled return. Ability-based, no gender or weight-loss gate.
Twice weekly with at least a day between; once weekly may follow same order. Two assisted sets starting 3/3 toward 5/5; add one rep to smaller set after targets met with control. Two controlled top sessions offer less help; easy may offer earlier.
Two extra lowering sets of one rep: about one second top hold and three-second descent. Assistance stays as needed. Replace existing pull-up/pulldown work; count all pulling exposure.
Check up to three reps, maximum three checks per local day. First accepted check counts as set one. Two or more checks finish the day even if final check succeeds. Failed harder check allows previous assistance: one attempt leaves set two, two finish the day. No extra lowerings on recovery days.
One position issue holds targets; two consecutive request a recheck. Pain/unsure pauses and saves entries. Return after 14 days offers 3/3 assisted with fresh check or 1/1 unassisted, no extra lowerings, and holds targets after return session.
Machine smallest reduction; feet less leg help; bands lighter repeatable combination. No universal load equivalence.
Optional supported platform lowering replaces one lowering set. Comfortable hang plus about three-second unsupported lowering suggests one first-rep attempt on a fresh day. Attempt replaces first set; if unsuccessful, rest and do remaining assisted set without extra lowerings.
After milestone: optional 1/1 toward 3/3, no extra lowerings. Stop forced reps, log completed work, Change assistance to recheck assisted route.
Review every eight qualifying sessions, not a completion deadline.
