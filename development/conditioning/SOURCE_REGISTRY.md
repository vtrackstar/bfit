# Conditioning source registry — revision 1
2026-11-26. Targeted web verification of records and relevant abstract/snippets or public guidance, not exhaustive full-text appraisal or medical review. Links below support the stated scope; numerical guide prescriptions are coaching decisions, not validated clinical cutoffs. 
| Claim area | Source / year | Scope / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Screening | [Riebe et al. 2015, Updating ACSM recommendations](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26473759/) | activity, disease/symptoms, desired intensity; not clearance |

| Activity targets | [WHO activity](https://www.who.int/initiatives/behealthy/physical-activity/), [CDC adults](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html/) | public-health goals, not starting dose |
| Adaptations | [Hellsten and Nyberg 2016 cardiovascular adaptations](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26756625/), [2024/25 mitochondria/capillaries meta-regression](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39390310/) | central/peripheral responses vary; no outcome guarantee |

| Hitting the wall | [2026 marathon-specific integrative review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42647332/) | prolonged endurance context, multifactorial |
