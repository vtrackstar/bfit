- mobility-post-sport: easy movement to downshift; optional comfortable static stretching after sport. Athletic warm-ups prioritize dynamic movement; brief optional static stretching followed by dynamic mobility and progressive sport-specific preparation. Do not imply immediate sitting is dangerous.
- mobility-foam-roller: retain manageable pressure and active follow-up; add screen protector versus lotion analogy as intentional technique, not a tissue mechanism; link user-supplied demo https://www.instagram.com/jump_startathletics/reel/DJXXe6hSPZz/ after media verification.

- mobility-sixty-minute: repair punctuation/order of shoulders and upper back row; optional shorter session; avoid guaranteed feelings of improvement.
- Upper-back education: distinguish available movement from endurance and comfort; shoulder blades move naturally. Preserve existing no-perfect-posture language.
