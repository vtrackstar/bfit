# Conditioning release safety and data integrity — 2026-11-26
Development preview only. No medical/clinical validation.
## Safety scope decision
After a reported warning, automated prescriptions remain paused; after reported pain, the provoking mode remains paused. Reader gets a clear route to qualified healthcare professional assessment and individualized return instructions, with separate logging of already-completed clinician-directed outside activity. Logging cannot clear a hold, replace a scheduled guide visit or qualify for progression. No "I feel better" or "cleared" button. This is deliberately log-only; an automated post-assessment restart is NOT implemented. If owner wants the latter, obtain appropriate clinical review and define individualized restrictions, provenance and restart protocol before implementing. AHA supports immediate cessation and medical review for exertional warning symptoms: https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/exercise-related-acute-cardiovascular-events-and-potential-deleterious-adaptations/top-things-to-know ; emergency recognition: https://www.heart.org/en/about-us/heart-attack-and-stroke-symptoms . These are public guidance, not validation of BFit software.
