What working with me can look like

Here's an example of how I'd use your training log, exercise feedback and check-ins to make a decision—not just change numbers on a spreadsheet.

Example: Your squat workout calls for three sets of eight. You consistently hit the first two sets, but the last drops to five reps and your video shows your depth changing as you fatigue.

What I'd check

I'd ask about your rest between sets, how hard the work felt, and the other lower-body training you did that week. I wouldn't assume you simply need to push harder.

What I'd change

If fatigue appears to be the main issue, I'd hold the weight steady, make sure you're resting enough, and adjust overlapping work instead of automatically adding more volume.

What we'd review

Over the next sessions, we'd look for consistent depth and repetitions across sets. If quality improves, we can build again. If it doesn't, I'd reassess the exercise and weekly workload.

Illustrative coaching scenario, not an actual client result or testimonial.
