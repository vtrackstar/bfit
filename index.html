Why are the guides free?

I believe everyone should have access to good training information, whether or not they can afford a coach. Getting started can be confusing, especially when you're hearing different advice from everyone. I want to give you a place to learn, try things for yourself and build confidence in your own decisions.

Even if we never work together, I hope these guides help you find a way to stay active that works for you.

Can I use more than one guide?

Yes! Choose the guides that help you work toward your goals. You don’t need to finish every program.

If you’re following Foundation Training, you can include push-up practice or pull-up practice in your existing workouts. Replace similar exercises instead of adding another full workout.

Training for your first 5K? Use Run Strong to learn more about running workouts. Leave time to recover between demanding leg workouts, runs and sports.

If you follow Weekend Warrior and want to focus on your glutes, use exercises from Build Your Glutes in place of similar leg exercises. Keep your other strength work and give your legs time to recover.

Gamer Fit movement breaks can fit around other training. If you’re unsure how to combine two plans, start with one and see how you recover.

Why pay for coaching if the guides are free?

If the free guides are helping you make progress on your own, keep using them. I wrote them so you can learn how to train without needing to pay for a coach.

When you work with me, I get to know your goals, your schedule and how your body responds to training. I build your workouts around you, review your progress each week and adjust your plan based on what you actually did. You can send me exercise videos for feedback and get help building eating habits that fit your routine.

For example, if your squats stop improving, I'll look at your workout records, technique and recovery to help you figure out what to change. If your work schedule changes, I'll help you reorganize your training instead of leaving you to figure it out alone.

You're paying for my time, experience and individual attention. If that's the kind of support you're looking for, you can explore one-on-one coaching → and see how I work with clients.
