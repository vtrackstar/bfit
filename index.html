Want a plan built around you?

I'm Bryan. I've spent eight years coaching strength and conditioning, working with athletes and everyday people who want to train with purpose. I start with your goals, training history, and schedule, then use your completed sessions and feedback to help decide what needs to change.

Free guides or personalized coaching?

The free BFit guides give you workouts, explanations, and tools to train independently. With personal coaching, your plan is built around you, your progress is reviewed, and adjustments are made with you as your needs change. Both have value; the difference is having a coach personally evaluate your training.

Both coaching options include individualized programming and thoughtful adjustments. Choose the level of feedback and collaboration that fits how you like to train.

PERSONALIZED GUIDANCE

Premium

See the difference in action

What happens when your training stops moving forward? Here's how the same challenge could look at each coaching level.

The situation

Your squat strength has stalled for several sessions. You're completing most of your workouts, but the final set is harder to finish and your technique becomes inconsistent.

With Premium

At your scheduled check-in, I'd review your recent sessions, effort, rest periods, and other lower-body training. If fatigue appears to be the issue, we'd hold the weight steady, adjust overlapping work, and set a clear target to review next time.

You get an informed adjustment and a clear next step.

With Elite

We'd start with the same analysis and adjustment. More frequent contact gives us additional chances to review a technique clip, discuss how the change felt, and refine the plan using your next training results.

You get that same direction, with closer feedback and follow-through.

Illustrative coaching scenario, not an actual client result, testimonial, or guarantee.
