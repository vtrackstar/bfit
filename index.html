Want help with your workouts?

I'm Bryan. I've coached strength and conditioning for eight years, working with athletes and people who want to get stronger and move better. I look at your goals, the training you can realistically fit into your week, and how you're responding before deciding what to change.

What's different about coaching?

These guides are free. You can choose a program, learn the exercises, and track your own progress. With coaching, I build your workouts, review what you complete, and help you make changes when something isn't working.

Premium gives you a plan and scheduled feedback. Elite has more frequent check-ins and chances to review your technique together.

PERSONAL COACHING

Premium

Your own training plan, with someone reviewing how it's going.

I'll build your workouts around your goals, equipment, and schedule, review your check-ins, and give feedback on selected exercise videos.

Good fit if: You're comfortable training on your own but want someone to handle the programming and help you make adjustments.

MORE FREQUENT COACHING

Elite

More check-ins and feedback throughout your training.

You get the same individualized plan, with more chances to review your training, look at exercise videos, and work through problems as they come up.

Good fit if: You prefer regular contact and want more chances to ask questions, review technique, and adjust your plan.

How I'd handle a training plateau

Here's how I'd handle the same problem with each plan.

The situation

You've been completing your squat workouts, but the last set has dropped from eight reps to five for the past few sessions. Your depth also changes when you get tired.

With Premium

At our scheduled check-in, I'd look at how hard the sets feel, how long you're resting, and what else you're doing for legs that week. If fatigue looks like the issue, I'd hold the weight steady, adjust some of the other work, and see how your next sessions go.

You know what we're changing and what we're checking next.

With Elite

I'd start with the same review and adjustment. With more regular feedback, we could look at another technique clip, check how the change went, and decide whether anything else needs changing.

We have more chances to check whether the adjustment helped.

Illustrative example, not a client testimonial.
