## Acceptance changes (proposed, not implemented)
P0 = release blocker; P1 = content revision; P2 = optional enhancement.
1. P1 Entry: offer Start a plan, Learn why, Resume; preview one very short workout and effort cues before the science. Keep all ten topics, not ten mandatory screens. Target a ~20–30% reduction in default visible prose, not total educational depth. 
2. P1 Put advanced ATP, oxygen deficit, threshold domains, VO2max detail, clinical McArdle distinction and study methodology in optional sections. Introduce plain explanation first. Keep practical safety visible.
3. P1 Remove repeated 'Take this into your workout' and quiz boilerplate; retain a short practical action when useful, optional comprehension prompts only for real misconceptions.
4. P1 Use first person for real coaching decisions, direct verbs and concrete cues; cut generic reassurance and formal wording (e.g. 'the intended stimulus' -> 'what this workout is meant to improve'). Replace technical labels in controls with plain wording, optionally define terms in context. Keep meaningful terms such as tempo, lactate, RPE.
5. P1 Ch1 distinguish cardiorespiratory fitness, maximal aerobic power (VO2max), endurance, muscular endurance, repeatability and movement economy. Define work capacity here as ability to handle/repeat physical work; not a universally standardized measure. Avoid 'just as much' equivalence and imply no single limiter can be diagnosed by feeling.
6. P1 Ch1 replace long adaptation paragraph with oxygen delivery/use chain (heart pumps blood, muscles receive and use oxygen, mitochondria help make ATP). Add concise health/everyday benefit; avoid heart/lungs 'get stronger' or guaranteed adaptation.
7. P1 Ch1 public-health target: WHO 150–300 moderate or 75–150 vigorous minutes/week or equivalent, strength >=2 days; any activity is useful. Clarify BFit effort categories are not exact public-health intensity labels; do not count all work or timer minutes as qualifying. Plan alone may not meet target. 
8. P1 Ch2 lead with everyday second wind, then ATP as optional named energy currency. Explain overlapping systems without implying anaerobic means no oxygen in body, a fixed crossover, or precise percentages. Clarify glycolysis breaks glucose/glycogen, oxidative uses carbohydrate/fat, phosphocreatine rapidly restores ATP.
9. P1 Ch2 distinguish ordinary perceived ease/warming up from measured oxygen uptake kinetics; mechanisms may contribute, not proof of second wind. Keep McArdle note optional and non-diagnostic. Dark urine/severe muscle symptoms warrant prompt medical attention, do not wait. 
10. P1 Ch2 conceptual diagram must show baseline nonzero energy demand and aerobic supply, rise to plateau only at sustainable constant workload, total demand vs oxidative supply same conceptual units, shaded gap labeled nonoxidative contribution/oxygen deficit conceptual; no timed or predicted response. Not a direct plot of subjective effort. 
11. P1 Ch2 warmup: exact cue for easy walking/bike and gradual increase; short visits adapt prep safely. Avoid universal five-minute rule. Move detailed fuel-use/fat-loss content to FAQ with short cross-link.
12. P1 Ch3 fatigue definition separate perceived fatigue from performance fatigability; explain local vs cardiorespiratory limitation as observations, not diagnosis. Add distinct response for ordinary exertion vs warning symptoms; do not imply breathlessness automatically poor fitness.
13. P1 Ch3 lactate: not the sole cause of burn/fatigue, used as fuel; no simplistic 'lactic acid causes burn' nor 'lactate has nothing to do with exercise fatigue'. DOMS distinct. 
14. P1 Ch3 recovery: aerobic ATP production contributes to phosphocreatine resynthesis; not synonymous with heart rate normalizing. Recovery can be active/passive; no claim that easy movement universally improves recovery. For novices on land: slow down, wait for comfortable breathing, extend once if appropriate, end challenging work if not ready. Swimming rest at wall.
15. P1 Ch3 keep single pacing graph; labels 'illustrative' and comparable conditions, controlled start vs early overpacing; avoid implying lower output = improved adaptation. Move matched longitudinal example to Ch9.
16. P1 Ch4 explain internal response vs external workload with one everyday comparison; teach a safe first effort check before zone theory. RPE 0 rest/10 maximal; BFit 2–3 easy, 4–5 steady, 6–7 challenging, 8 hard, 9–10 not prescribed. Distinguish Run Strong zone ranges and CDC numeric intensity categories explicitly, do not translate zone numbers literally. Full sentences vs short phrases practical approximations; no singing test required, no talk test on unsafe activities or swimming.
17. P1 Ch4 create short activity-specific cues for walking, bike, row, elliptical, stairs, marching, swim and circuits; add machine-use/demo routes only if verified. One adjustment at a time. Stable chair/step/bench; safe controls; no phone use while moving unsafely.
18. P1 Ch4 make watch lag/drift optional explanations with conceptual axes and no invented measured data; distinguish HR vs workload, medication/device limitations, heat and hydration. Stop for concerning symptoms. Use HR lag paper cautiously: retrospective HIIT recovery, not direct validation of every illustrative curve.
19. P1 Ch4 zone depth: concise 3-zone/5-zone convention and thresholds; no direct map, individualized physiology. Avoid repeating Run Strong's zone cards, workout catalog, pace/race content. Explain VO2max once and link; no max test. Cite ACSM/ESSA terminology and CDC talk test distinctly.
20. P1 Ch5 main structure: steady vs repeated controlled work; then choose purpose; optional tempo, HIIT, sprint interval and fartlek definitions. Distinguish HIIT relative high intensity vs generic intervals; no mandatory HIIT. Keep 2026 near-VO2max result acute, not proof of long-term superior adaptation. Add longitudinal evidence 115-trial comparison and umbrella review.
21. P1 Ch5 fix work/rest examples: 6x1:2 = 6 work +12 rest=18 main; 3x2:2 =6+6=12 main. Same work time, different rest AND bout duration, cannot isolate interval length; teach single variable comparison 4x30s+90s vs 4x30s+30s (2 work in both, 6 vs 2 rest, 8 vs 4 main) as educational only. All include final recovery; label warm/cool separate. 
22. P1 Ch5 MetCon: broad nonstandard term, circuits can train multiple qualities, local fatigue may limit cardiovascular stimulus; distinguish familiar moderate circuit from hard conditioning. Explain four stations x30s work+30s rest x2 = 8 main, 4 work, 4 recovery; stable setup, no speed contest, alternatives/demos. Optional anaerobic capacity definition (nonoxidative energy supply during hard work), not separate automated path or exact measure.
23. P1 Ch6 plain two goal labels: 'Keep going longer' / 'Recover between efforts' alongside established names Build Stamina/Improve Work Capacity. Preserve user choice, same early base possible. Show first activity, short bouts option, days/time includes preparation. Avoid requirement for logs a new reader cannot provide; explicitly unknown vs prior self-report; no max testing.
24. P0 Ch6 safety entry: known relevant disease, symptoms, recent activity, intended intensity and restrictions are distinct from training readiness. No blanket clearance claim, no exercise recommendation that overrides medical restriction; uncertain suitability -> appropriate advice. Standard stop and emergency wording: chest pain/pressure suggestive of heart attack, fainting, severe breathlessness or other emergency symptoms -> call local emergency number immediately; do not wait to see whether it passes. Nonurgent symptoms -> stop and appropriate evaluation before returning. AHA/CDC and ACSM screening references. No symptom flag clearing by questionnaire; clinician-directed restart pathway must be defined before release. 
25. P0 Ch6 swim: water competent, lifeguarded pool and buddy, familiar length, safe rest at wall, no breath holding or open-water program; no hard aquatic plans, no timer mid-swim. Add Red Cross and CDC links. 
26. P1 Ch6 make 'choose your level' actually 'how we choose your starting point'. Give first short workout under 5 continuous min, warm/cool included; don't assume every session 15 min. Keep ordinary circuits available before specialization; no level ranking.
27. P0 Ch7 no unsupported timer/progress claims. Show intended effort and first physical action; actual time distinct from planned; confirm before recording, no credit for locked/background uncertainty. Need safe early-end/cooldown, extension, pause/resume/skip, edit/undo provenance and next-day recovery chronology enforced. Avoid default next-day 'normal' or user selection to unlock hard work. 
28. P1 Ch7 examples verified arithmetically: 5+5+5=15; 5+15+5=25; 3x(30+90)+600=16min; 4x(60+120)+600=22min, 3x=19min. Keep short-on-time vs fatigue distinction; shorter time cap not failure or full prescribed completion. Include example of 1–3 minute easy bouts when appropriate.
29. P0 Ch8 scheduling claims conditional on engine: distinguish counted workload from comparable readiness, external hard day replacement not equivalent to intervals, dedup outside activity, no required catchup, schedule recovery and optional sessions. One day alternate only if prepared. Existing plan kept rather than arbitrary reduction. 
30. P1 Ch8 combine guides with verified README paths; add Keep Moving 50+ (appropriate mobility/health goals) and Gamer Fit (short easy movement) only when relevant; no duplication or promised shared logs. Sample schedule strength priority vs conditioning priority; protect important session quality; separate demanding training when feasible, not universal rest-hour mandate. Explain concurrent evidence scope; no guarantee of zero interference.
31. P1 Ch9 single meaningful example each for endurance and repeatability; rpm decline 12.5% vs 2.5% arithmetic correct, comparable settings/time/effort/recovery, no proof of VO2max; define rpm, distinguish speed/resistance and output. Add actual work vs recovery vs total separately, no missing numbers invented.
32. P0 Ch9 review: four-week attendance denominator only once planned slots tracked; separate optional/external/prior and unknown swimming time; cycle dates, matched comparisons, recovery and decision rationale. Until implemented no personalized chart/claim; fictional example explicitly labeled. 
33. P0 Ch9 data: local browser save/backup/restore only after verified; clearly no accounts/sync; storage failure/clearing warning. Test versioned import, corruption, private browsing, reset, actual downloads on device. 
34. P1 Ch10 FAQ answers 1–3 concise sentences and deep link to primary chapter. Consolidate second wind/lactate/HIIT/Zone2/rest explanations, keep EPOC and hitting wall as unique FAQ; marathon-specific evidence, distinguish unusual short-exercise symptoms. Fat oxidation relative share vs absolute rate vs long-term body fat; no universal crossover graph. 
35. P1 Ch10 EPOC modest/contextual, no universal calorie estimate; sweating is thermoregulation, no forced dehydration; cardio compatibility qualified; modality transfer limited; don't use newer research solely because new.
36. P1 Add minimal glossary or contextual terms (ATP, aerobic, anaerobic, RPE, rpm, watts, interval, tempo, work capacity, recovery, VO2max). Keep science expandable and human voice. 
37. P0 Evidence: build source registry by claim with author/year/title/link and relevance/limitations, verify remaining IDs (Bishop 21846163, AHA warmup, GeneReviews, heat DOI, specific HR lag claim, cardiovascular adaptation, sleep/heat, medications/wearables, anaerobic capacity/SIT/RST, movement economy). Remove unverified/mismatched citations rather than present as validated. Verify link targets/canonical routes in final HTML. 
38. P0 Run Strong cross-check: actual main headings include five zones, pace, workouts, aerobic power/VO2, thresholds, energy systems, economy, schedule and decisions. Keep shared science consistent (not exact RPE mapping); distinct practical purpose, no copied zone card architecture, race/event prescriptions or redundant FAQ. Final page-level comparison after implementation; no edits to Run Strong proposed.
39. P0 Implementation gates: current 85 checks were isolated JS/combined workflow, not full browser/CI. Remaining engine gaps (hard eligibility, initial/slot mismatch, reduced-dose canonical, same-day recovery, unresolved warning, weekly scheduler, early cooldown, logging and review) must be addressed/tested before publication; no automated hard prescription if safety ambiguous.
