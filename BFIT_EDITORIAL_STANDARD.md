# BFit editorial and release standard

Current owner-approved policy, 2026-11-27. Applies to new guides, workout plans, milestones, the library and the hub. Dated audits are historical; use current main, README and this standard before editing.

## Beginner-first voice
- Assume the reader has no fitness background and may want only a workout. Put the next physical action first. Give setup, what to do, how much, when to rest and when to stop. Explain a new term immediately with an example; make science optional when not necessary to act.
- Write as a coach speaking naturally to a person. Avoid needless jargon, clinical-sounding commands, filler, sales pressure, unexplained acronyms and overly formal or repetitive wording. Use observable cues (e.g. chest and hips move together) before estimated repetitions remaining for complete beginners. Do not promise outcomes or exact timelines.
- Explain equipment visually in words, including how to recognize a machine, safe entry/exit and alternatives. Do not imply a machine or alternative is identical when mechanics differ. Retain technical vocabulary where learning it is useful, e.g. dead hang or hip rotation.
- Separate ordinary manageable exertion from pain or warning symptoms; preserve safety pauses and medical scope. General fitness guidance is not injury rehabilitation, medical clearance or diagnosis. Preserve evidence qualifications. Do not introduce new physiological claims without checking sources. Dynamic preparation before athletic activity, optional brief static stretching followed by dynamic work before activity, optional static stretching afterward; no recovery guarantee. Eccentric work is controlled muscle lengthening under tension, not all slow movement. Count existing strength work.

## Product boundaries
- Guide: education plus practical entry; plan: workout-first independent route; milestone: one achievement; library: look up one movement. Each can work alone. Cross-link when relevant, never require reading every guide. Replacing similar training is preferable to stacking full routines. Movement breaks and dedicated practice serve different goals. Keep Foundations first; span both desktop columns only for odd guide count. Keep specialty plans and milestones horizontally scrollable, with visible controls on overflow. Keep hub back-to-top and all return links.
- Free content is complete enough to use independently. Explain why free before collection, why paid coaching in Meet your coach. Paid value is individual attention, assessment and adjustments, not withholding knowledge. Preserve current Premium $199 and Elite $499 and paused intake until owner explicitly changes it. Twitch stays off hub until requested; existing guide links are not globally removed. Use pale blue headings and green accent consistently with existing theme.
- Nutrition: explain how coaching helps, then demonstrate the nutrition dashboard, then distinguish coaching, nutritionist and registered dietitian scope. Preserve legal/medical scope and avoid claims about regulated titles that have not been verified for the relevant jurisdiction.

## Catalog and release
- Preserve BFIT IDs, public paths, anchors, engine values and existing saved history. Active editorial master, approved-only copy, catalog.json and embedded snapshot must reconcile before release. Check row identity and approval states; change only intended cells; verify readback. Never publish draft or unvetted media. An exercise link is not proof of demo verification. When exercises share equipment but differ in movement, explain both.
- Keep edits small and isolated; guard main updates against exact parent. Document changed files, purpose, commit, tests, deployment, residual issues and rollback in current implementation ledger. A successful Pages deployment alone is not successful regression. Fix failures rather than weaken substantive tests; update stale copy/structure assertions to preserve behavior checks. Do not claim a physical device, manual screen-reader, first-reader or provider test unless performed.
- Before completion: full regression at latest exact SHA, relevant production workflows, browser mobile and desktop layout, keyboard navigation, return links, saved-data paths, timer/feedback/backup where applicable, catalog parity and link integrity. Use READER_WALKTHROUGH.md for manual validation; record manual gaps separately. Review all changes for unintentional scope or algorithm changes. Keep the owner informed of real blockers and estimated implementation progress, not time estimates.
